I had a domain in cpanel which also had smtp or mail setup on that same domain. The website was also hosted on the Cpanel.
But I had to move/point that main domain to a VPS IP but keep the mail/SMTP on cpanel.

Here is how i managed it. Take domain 'ghosthost.online':
When i say main domain that mean without subdomain like www.ghosthost.online, api.ghosthost.online, app.ghosthost.online etc. these are cname or sub domains.


* So first, My main domain was pointing to Cpanel's IP address
    I Changed A record or @ of main domain to point to VPS IP.
* My mail.ghosthost.online cname was pointing to A record or @ or main domain
    I Changed it to A record and Pointed it to Cpanel's IP address
* My MX record was pointing to my main domain
    I Change it to point to my main domain or @

I did not change anything else.

Give it 1 to 4 hours, it will work (mine took 1-2 minutes)
